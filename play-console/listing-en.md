# GONDUT — Google Play store listing

App name:
GONDUT

Short description:
Capture your thoughts, connect your ideas, and reflect offline.

Full description:
GONDUT is an offline-first space for capturing notes and connecting related ideas in a visual graph.

• Write notes with formatting and automatic saving.
• Organize each note in a tree that you can create and edit yourself.
• Write by hand, then review and edit recognized text before adding it to a note.
• Try on-device AI process drafts from written notes or recognized handwriting.
• Record voice memos and create drafts with on-device processing.
• Back up and restore your notes and trees using a file.
• Choose an app-wide pastel theme and interface language.

Handwriting recognition, voice recognition, and AI-generated drafts are being tested. Results can be inaccurate, so please review them against the original. Voice recordings can be up to five minutes long.

Your notes and voice processing stay on your device. GONDUT does not require an account or provide cloud synchronization.
