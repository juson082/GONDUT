# GONDUT — Google Play store listing

App name:
GONDUT

Short description:
Write notes and grow your ideas with little companions, offline.

Full description:
GONDUT is an offline-first space for writing notes and organizing your ideas in a tree graph. As your ideas grow, little characters explore the branches alongside you.

• Write and format notes with automatic saving.
• Create your own tree graphs and move, edit, and connect branches freely.
• Add titles and details to individual branches.
• Write and draw by hand in notes and graphs, preserving your original strokes.
• Organize your notes and graphs in folders.
• Meet little characters that wander along branches and react when you tap them.
• Show or hide the characters in settings.
• Choose a plant-inspired theme and your preferred interface language.
• Back up and restore your notes and trees using a file.

Your notes and graphs are stored on your device. GONDUT does not require an account or provide cloud synchronization.
